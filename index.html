Something else?

Is this a signal service?

No. The indicators map targets, zones, lines and stops. You decide what to do with them, and you need your own strategy for them to be useful.

What is PoT?

Probability of Touch: an estimate of the chance price reaches the marked target, computed from its distance and current volatility.

Do the indicators repaint?

No. Higher-timeframe data is read from closed bars only, and lines and zones are fixed once drawn.

Do I need a paid TradingView plan?

The free plan shows two indicators per chart. To run more than two at once you need a paid TradingView plan.

How fast do I get access?

Invites are sent by hand to the TradingView username you give at checkout, normally the same day.

Which markets and timeframes?

Any symbol and any timeframe on TradingView. They were built on index futures.
